Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to secure your child's admission at Sanskar Tirth Thakar

Step 1: Enquiry Form

Fill out the admission enquiry form available online or at the school office to begin the admission process.

Step 2: Fill Admission Form

Complete the admission form with all required details and submit it to the school office.

Important Note

Admission will be confirmed only after the Principal's Interaction and fee payment + documents submission.

Step 3: Interaction with Principal

Schedule and attend an interaction session with the Principal for admission confirmation.

Step 4: Submit Required Documents

Submit all required documents including birth certificate, ID proofs, and transfer certificate.

Step 5: Fee Payment

Complete the fee payment through bank transfer, online payment, or at the school office to confirm admission.
