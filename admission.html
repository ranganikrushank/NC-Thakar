Welcome to NC Thakkar Education Campus

We are delighted to welcome prospective students and their families to NC Thakkar Education Campus. Admissions are now open for the academic year 2026-27. Our school offers a nurturing environment where every child can excel academically, socially, and emotionally. We invite you to explore our programs, visit our campus, and become part of our vibrant learning community. Let's shape a brighter future together through quality education and holistic development.

Rules & Regulations

Important guidelines for students and parents to ensure a harmonious learning environment

School Uniform

All students must wear the prescribed school uniform on all working days. Proper ID cards must be displayed at all times.

Punctuality

Students must arrive at school before 8:00 AM. Late arrivals will be recorded and may require written explanation from parents.

Attendance

Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory for examination eligibility. Parents must inform the school about any absence in advance.

Mobile Phones

Mobile phones are not allowed in the classroom. Students may carry phones for transport purposes only, to be surrendered at the office.

Fee Payment

All fees must be paid by the due date. Late payment may attract additional charges. Fee structure is available at the office.

Discipline

Students must maintain discipline and respect towards teachers, staff, and fellow students. Bullying and ragging are strictly prohibited.

Homework

Daily homework is an integral part of learning. Students must complete and submit all assignments on time. Regular practice is encouraged.

Health & Safety

Students with medical conditions must inform the school office. Regular health check-ups and vaccination records must be maintained.

Examinations

All students must appear for all scheduled examinations. Absence requires a valid reason with supporting documentation from parents.
